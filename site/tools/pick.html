Pick helper

Type the cards in your pack, and optionally what you've taken so far. It ranks the pack by the podcast hosts' grades and how well each card fits the colors your best picks point to. Drafting on Arena? The live pick helper reads the pack for you.

Simulated with mulligan, an open-source Magic: The Gathering Limited simulator. Card data from Scryfall; real-game comparisons from 17Lands' public data. Not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast. Built by Nathan Knapp · LinkedIn.
